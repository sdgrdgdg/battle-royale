SEASON 1

GLOBAL LAUNCH • 2018

This fan recreation is inspired by the early Battlelands Royale gameplay loop: choose a landing point, parachute onto the arena, grab a weapon, survive the storm and become the last battler standing.

The recreation uses its own procedural art and code. It does not use the original game's files or assets.
